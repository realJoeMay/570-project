    • We are glad to have you visit our church and website! 

      Welcome to Our Lady of the Gulf Catholic Church! 
      We are overjoyed to have you join our faith community and extend our warmest greetings. As part of our spiritual family, you are an integral part of our mission to grow together in faith and fellowship. Whether you seek solace, guidance, or a sense of belonging, our church is here to support you. please explore the resources available to help you feel at home, and don't hesitate to reach out with any questions or needs on our faith journey.
